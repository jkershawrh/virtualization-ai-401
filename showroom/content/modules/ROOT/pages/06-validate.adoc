= 6. VALIDATE

Exercise three conditions through the same contract:

|===
|Condition |Infrastructure |Application |AI path |Decision
|Healthy migration |PASS |PASS |PASS |`ALLOW_REVIEW`
|AI dependency outage |PASS |PASS |FAIL |`ABSTAIN`
|Application failure after platform success |PASS |FAIL |PASS |`REFUSE`
|===

Do not equate infrastructure completion with service recovery. When the AI
dependency is unavailable, preserve degraded behavior and do not fabricate an
explanation or claim model participation.
