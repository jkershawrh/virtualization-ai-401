= Modernize VMs with Governed AI

This 90–120 minute level-301 lab starts from the completed 201 VM-to-AI
contract. You will declare identity, network, placement, observability, and
authority policy; compare it with independent observations; exercise
`ALLOW_REVIEW`, `REFUSE`, and `ABSTAIN`; and reclaim every candidate resource.

The model is advisory. It cannot run tools, change the VM, migrate workloads,
approve evidence, certify the lab, or promote artifacts. Factory fixtures are
`REHEARSAL`; only a complete current-session target receipt may say `LIVE`.

== Deliverables

* one versioned request and policy;
* allowed, refused, abstained, and model-unavailable records;
* bounded decision counters (not a platform benchmark);
* a named human accept/reject worksheet; and
* before/after resource inventories showing zero labeled residue.
