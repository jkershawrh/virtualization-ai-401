= 1. OBSERVE

Record the target OpenShift, OpenShift Virtualization, namespace, VM/VMI,
network, storage, application, and AI dependency identities. Mark each fact
`LIVE`, `REHEARSAL`, or `OFFLINE`, and retain its timestamp and digest.

Do not infer model, provider, Intel placement, CPU allocation, or utilization.
Unknown facts remain unknown. Confirm the adapter has no cluster credential and
cannot perform automated remediation. Factory fixtures are `REHEARSAL`.
